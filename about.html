About

A Mysuru studio, run by the person who builds it.

Studio AXD is a website and automation studio in Mysuru, Karnataka, started in 2026 by Anmol Prasad. We build websites, WhatsApp automation, lead capture and AI assistants for local businesses, then look after them every month. You deal with the founder directly, from the first demo to the monthly care.

How we work

  • You see a working demo before you pay for anything
  • No fixed packages: a one-time setup fee and a monthly fee, sized to your business
  • Everything is tested on real phones before it goes live
  • WhatsApp replies can be in English and Kannada
  • Your domain, your number and your customer data stay yours

  • Web and marketing agencies that need a build partner
