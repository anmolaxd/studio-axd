What we believe

Technology should take work away, not add a new screen.

Most small businesses have been sold software that nobody uses after a month. We would rather build less, build it around how you already work, and stay to make sure it keeps working.

01

Show it working before asking for money

Every conversation starts with a working demo on your phone and past work you can open. The demo is free; the build is quoted in writing.

02

Start from the business, not the tool

We ask what happens when a customer messages, who calls them back and where it is written down. The technology comes after.

03

A person stays in charge

AI drafts, sorts and answers within rules you set. Anything that matters, from medical questions to refunds, goes to a person.

04

Plain language, plain prices

No jargon in quotes. A setup fee and a monthly fee, sized to your business, written down before any work starts.

05

Yours to keep

Your domain, your WhatsApp number and your customer data stay yours, even if you stop working with us.

Why founder-led

No account managers. No hand-offs.

The person who listens to your problem is the person who designs and builds the answer. Nothing is lost between a salesperson, a project manager and a developer.
