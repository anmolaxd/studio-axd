What moves the price

  • Number of pages, services and branches
  • Number of languages (English, Kannada, Hindi)
  • Online booking, payments or membership plans
  • Connecting to software you already use
  • How many WhatsApp messages a month you expect

What the monthly fee covers

  • Hosting and security updates
  • Keeping the WhatsApp assistant running and checked
  • Fixing anything that breaks
  • Small updates such as prices, timings, photos and offers (up to 1 hour a month; bigger changes are quoted)
  • Support on WhatsApp and phone

Payment terms and timelines

  • Payment. 50% of the setup fee to start, 50% at go-live. The monthly fee starts from go-live.
  • Yearly option. Pay the monthly fee for 10 months and get 12.
  • Paid directly by you, at cost. Domain name (usually ₹800 to ₹1,500 a year), Meta's WhatsApp message fees, and payment gateway fees if you take online payments. We tell you the expected amount before you start.
  • Timeline. A website usually goes live 1 to 3 weeks after we receive your content (text, photos, prices). A WhatsApp assistant usually takes 2 to 3 weeks, including Meta's approval of your business number.
  • Stopping. You can stop the monthly plan with 30 days' notice. Your domain, content and customer data stay yours.
  • Free. The demo is free. Builds, sample sites and setups are always paid.

Questions

Why do you give ranges and not fixed prices?

A three-chair salon and a coaching centre with five branches need different amounts of work. We give you one exact price in writing after a short demo and a chat, before any work starts.

Is there a free trial or a free website?

No free builds. The live demo is free: you can try our WhatsApp assistant on your own phone and see past work before you decide.

Do I pay WhatsApp or payment gateway fees to you?

No. Meta's WhatsApp message fees, your domain and payment gateway fees are paid by you directly to those companies, at their price. We tell you the expected amount before you start.

Can I pay yearly?

Yes. Pay the monthly fee for 10 months and get 12.

Do I own the website and the data?

Yes. Your domain, your WhatsApp number, your content and your customer data stay yours.
