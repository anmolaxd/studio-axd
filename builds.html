Client · Aashrya Educational Foundation

An online scholarship exam for an education foundation

We build and run the technology for Aashrya Educational Foundation. The main piece is the exam platform for its Anmol Fellowship Programme: students in Classes 8 and 9 sit a supervised scholarship test at home, on the phone or computer their family already has.

  • Parents sign in with a one-time code, give consent and pay online
  • Each student gets their own paper, and every answer is saved the moment it is chosen
  • Results, ranks and certificates, with staff tools to run exam day
  • English and Kannada, with all data kept in India

We also make the programme's pitch decks for schools and partners, and its budget sheets.
