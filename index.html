How the WhatsApp assistant works

From message to reply, in seconds.

  1. 01

    Message arrives

    A customer writes to your WhatsApp Business number, any time of day.

  2. 02

    AI reads it

    It spots the language, what they want and how urgent it is.

  3. 03

    Reply goes out

    A short, polite reply in the same language, from your business name.

  4. 04

    You get the lead

    Name, need and a suggested next step, ready for you or your team.
