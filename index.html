What we build

  2. 02

One example: the WhatsApp assistant

01

A customer writes in.

Any hour, any language. If the reply comes hours later, they have often called someone else.

02

The AI reads it.

It works out the language, what they want and how urgent it is. It never invents prices, timings or doctor names.

03

A reply goes out in seconds.

Short, polite and in the customer's own language, from your business name, on the official WhatsApp Business platform.

04

You get the lead.

Name, need and a suggested next step land in your list, so your team only steps in to close.
