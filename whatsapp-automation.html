Questions

Does it replace my staff?

No. It answers the common questions and passes serious enquiries, such as bookings and anything medical or unclear, to your team.

Will it invent prices or advice?

No. It uses only the facts you approve. If it is unsure, it tells the customer your team will call.

Which languages?

English and Kannada. We can proofread the Kannada with you before launch.

What do I need?

A WhatsApp Business account and a number for the assistant. Replies are free inside 24 hours of the customer messaging you, and we explain the other WhatsApp rules at setup.
