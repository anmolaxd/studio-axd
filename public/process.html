The five steps

01

Live demo

15 minutes, in person in Mysuru or on a video call. You try a working system on your own phone and see past work, so you know exactly what you would get.

02

Scope and price

We ask how your business runs today: where enquiries come from, who replies, what is written down where. Then one exact price in writing. No fixed packages; it is sized to your business.

03

Build

You send text, photos, prices and rules. You approve the design and every automatic answer before anything goes live.

04

Launch

Your domain, your number, tested on real phones. Google Search Console and Analytics set up, and your staff shown how it works.

Look after it

A monthly plan for hosting, fixes and small updates, with error alerts so we hear about a problem before your customers do.
