The problem

Gyms rarely lose members in an argument. They lose them in silence.

A membership ends, nobody calls, and the member simply does not come back. Meanwhile trial enquiries from ads wait in Instagram messages until the next morning.

Gym OS puts members, renewals, trainers and payments in one place, and the WhatsApp assistant books trials while the floor is busy.
