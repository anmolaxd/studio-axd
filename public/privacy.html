Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 November 2026

This policy explains what Studio AXD ("we") collects when you visit this website or contact us, and how we use it. Studio AXD is run by Anmol Prasad, Mysuru, Karnataka, India.

What we collect

  • When you contact us by WhatsApp or email: your name, phone number or email address, and what you write to us.
  • When you visit this site: we use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages and buttons (WhatsApp, email, phone) are used. It sets cookies and collects anonymous usage data such as pages viewed, device type and approximate location, as described in Google's privacy policy. You can block it with any ad blocker or the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. The site has no advertising cookies. The project form at the bottom of each page sends what you type (your need, message, name, business and the WhatsApp number or email you give) to us as a private Telegram message, so we can reply. The website itself does not keep a copy. If you choose "message us on WhatsApp" instead, it opens WhatsApp with your message filled in. Our host (Cloudflare), Telegram and our font provider (Google Fonts) may log basic technical data such as IP address, as described in their own policies.

How we use it

Only to reply to you, prepare a sample or quote you asked for, and deliver work you have agreed to. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Client projects

When we build a system for your business, any customer messages or data it handles belong to you. We use them only to run and fix that system, and we agree how long they are kept with you in writing.

How long we keep it

Conversations with prospective clients are kept for up to 12 months after our last contact, unless we start working together. Project records are kept for as long as the project runs and as required by Indian law.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing hello@studioaxd.com. We will reply within 30 days. We handle personal data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
