The problem

Admission season is won on the phone at 9 pm.

Parents compare three or four centres in an evening. The one that answers about fees, batches and timings first usually gets the visit.

After admission, the same office is chasing fees, marking attendance on paper and correcting hundreds of answer sheets by hand. We connect all of it, so the office does less and parents hear more.

Questions

Before you ask.

Do we have to buy everything at once?

No. Most centres start with the website and WhatsApp assistant before admission season, then add the ERP, Smart Marking, study material or online mock tests when they need them.

How much does it cost?

Every centre pays a one-time setup fee and a monthly fee, sized to the number of students, branches and parts you choose. You get one exact price in writing after a short demo, before any work starts.

How accurate is the AI marking?

We do not promise a number before testing on your papers. Smart Marking always starts with a paid accuracy test on your own answer sheets, checked against your teachers' marks.

Who owns the data?

You do. Your domain, WhatsApp number, student records, marks and test results stay yours.
