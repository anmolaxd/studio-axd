The problem

Patients message when they are worried, which is rarely during clinic hours.

A question about timings at 10 pm, an appointment request on Sunday, a price question while the doctor is with a patient. The front desk replies the next day, and by then the patient has booked elsewhere or forgotten.

We set up the clinic's enquiries so every patient gets a reply at once, clinical questions always reach a person, and booked patients get reminded.

Safety rules

What the assistant never does.

  • It never diagnoses or gives medical advice. Clinical questions go to your staff.
  • It never invents prices, timings or doctor names. It uses only the facts you approve.
  • It never shares one patient's details with another.
  • When unsure, it says your team will call, and alerts them.

Questions

Before you ask.

Will it give medical advice?

No. It answers practical questions such as timings, location and approved fees. Anything clinical is passed to your staff.

Can it book into our existing clinic software?

Often yes, if your software allows connections. If not, requests go to a shared list your front desk confirms.

Is patient data safe?

Messages run on the official WhatsApp Business platform and are stored only in accounts you own. We agree in writing what is kept and for how long.
