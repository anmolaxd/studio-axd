The problem

Your best people spend hours on work a machine could draft.

Reading forms, copying details into sheets, sorting tickets, writing the same replies. It is necessary work, and it eats the hours meant for customers.

AI can now do most of the reading and drafting. The skill is in deciding what it may do alone, what a person must check, and what happens when something unusual comes in. That is what we design.

What you get

Hours back, with a person in charge.

  • Reading documents and emails and pulling out the data you need
  • Drafting replies and reports for your team to check and send
  • Sorting support tickets by topic and urgency
  • Rules that decide what the AI may do alone and what needs a person
  • Error alerts, so a failure never goes unnoticed

Who it suits

Teams buried in the same task every day.

  • Shopify and online stores with a busy support inbox
  • Offices handling forms, invoices or applications
  • Agencies that repeat the same reports
  • Teams that already use Google Sheets and email
