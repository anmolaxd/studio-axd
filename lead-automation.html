The problem

Leads do not disappear. They wait in five places until they go cold.

A form on the website, a comment on an ad, a WhatsApp at night, a missed call. Each one is somebody ready to buy, and each one sits somewhere different.

We put every enquiry in one list, alert the right person straight away and remind the team about anyone not yet called.

What you get

One list. Nobody forgotten.

  • Website and ad form leads saved to a Google Sheet or your CRM
  • An instant alert by WhatsApp, Telegram or email when a lead arrives
  • Missing phone numbers and emails caught before the lead is saved
  • Follow-up reminders for leads nobody has called yet
  • A simple status column: New, Called, Booked, Done

Who it suits

Teams paying for leads.

  • Businesses running Meta or Google ads
  • Agencies handling leads for clients
  • Clinics, coaching centres and real estate teams
  • Anyone who copies enquiries into a notebook today
